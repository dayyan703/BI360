# Business Insight 360 📊

## Overview
Business Insight 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze business performance across different departments. It provides insights into Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain through visualizations, KPIs, and interactive reports.

## Dashboard Views

- **Finance:** Tracks financial performance, profitability, and key financial metrics.
- **Sales:** Analyzes sales performance across customers, products, and regions.
- **Marketing:** Provides insights into marketing performance and profitability.
- **Supply Chain:** Monitors forecast accuracy, demand, and supply-related metrics.
- **Executive View:** Presents an overall summary of business performance.
